LOW-RISE CONDOMINIUMS

Rank-Zoned Housing · Central Public Core · Symmetrical Core Planning

  • LocationMagadi Road, Rajajinagar, Bengaluru, India
  • ProgrammeBachelor of Architecture — Design Studio
  • ScopeUrban Master Redevelopment, Rank-Based Typology Zoning, Core Planning, Microclimate Siting
  • ToolsAutodesk Revit, SketchUp, AutoCAD, Adobe Photoshop, Lumion, DesignBuilder

A rank-zoned community housing redevelopment for police personnel on Magadi Road.

The proposal replaces outdated infrastructure with a tiered master plan catering to multiple ranks—from senior officer villas to constable multi-family blocks. A centralized shared park connects all rank clusters to encourage community cohesion.

01. Site & Urban Redevelopment

Infill redevelopment within an established grid. Siting access off Magadi Road consolidates vehicle movement to the perimeter, reserving internal streets for shaded pedestrian routes, green play areas, and communal squares.

1 / 2

02. Typology Massing & Symmetrical Core

Vertical programming hierarchy. The block transitions from management offices and entry lobbies at ground level to repeatable 1BHK and 2BHK units on middle levels, culminating in larger 3BHK senior suites on the top floor.

03. Floor Plans & Unit Distribution

1 / 3

Each typical level mirrors units across a central circulation and service shaft, ensuring natural light and cross-ventilation in all living spaces.

04. Envelope Detailing & Structural Section

Floor-to-floor heights of 3.2 meters maintain vertical alignment across all storeys. Deep recessed balconies provide solar protection along primary façades while keeping flank elevations solid to control thermal gain.

05. Academic & Design Takeaways

  • 1. Multi-Tiered Typology Planning Zoning by rank allowed varied residential configurations without breaking the structural rhythm of the overall block.
  • 2. Integrated Community Open Space Placing shared parks at the convergence of all housing types prevented spatial segregation among different ranks.
  • 3. Symmetrical Core Efficiency Organizing unit layouts symmetrically around a single stair and MEP shaft minimized circulation waste and simplified services.
  • 4. Solar Protection via Massing Recessing windows behind continuous balcony bands cut direct solar exposure on residential units.
