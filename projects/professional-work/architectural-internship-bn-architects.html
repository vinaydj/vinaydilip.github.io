Architectural Internship

Hospitality · Commercial Fit-Outs · Luxury Residential · On-Site Execution

  • FirmB+N Architects · Bangalore, India
  • PositionArchitectural Intern
  • TimelineJune 2018 – July 2019
  • ScopeGFC Working Drawings, 3D Spatial Visualization, Material Boards, Site Coordination
  • ToolsAutodesk AutoCAD, SketchUp, Lumion, Adobe Photoshop

Engaged across 8 major national projects spanning hospitality, commercial workspaces, and luxury residences.

This tenure covered every stage of architectural execution—from concept sketches, mood boards, and 3D visualization to Good-for-Construction (GFC) drawings, MEP coordination, and site progress audits. Featured here are three flagship hospitality and commercial projects: Pune brewpubs (Toit and The Bier Library) and corporate/residential developments (Vaishnavi Serene, Ventura, and Embassy Boulevard).

01. Toit Brewpub — Pune

Rustic vernacular hospitality design. Toit’s architectural character centers on an outdoor two-story courtyard building featuring pitched clay-tiled roofs, exposed rough-cut granite masonry, and custom wrought-iron balustrades. A key design element was building the primary indoor dining volume around an existing mature tree, preserving natural canopy cover directly inside the dining hall under steel roof trusses.

1 / 7

02. The Bier Library — Pune

Industrial-chic microbrewery and reflecting pool courtyard. In contrast to Toit's rustic masonry, The Bier Library embraces an industrial language featuring vertical charred-timber screens, exposed concrete frames, and a central linear reflecting water channel illuminated from beneath to double as a passive evaporative cooling element.

1 / 4

03. Vaishnavi Serene — Marketing Office Fit-Out

High-end commercial interior within a precast shell. Retrofitting a temporary marketing lounge inside an existing clubhouse framework required custom acoustic slatted timber ceilings, terrazzo flooring with brass inlays, and modular glass partition walls designed to be relocated post-possession.

1 / 5

04. Ventura — Mock-Up Show Residence

Turnkey interior design and visualization for real estate show flats. Developed dual design schemes for bedrooms and living spaces, producing construction drawing sets for bespoke millwork, concealed electrical conduits, and modular sanitary fittings.

1 / 13

05. Embassy Boulevard — Luxury Villa

High-end private residential estate in Yelahanka, Bangalore. Designed across three levels around a central triple-height atrium with structural glass walls facing private garden courtyards. Detailing spanned cantilevered floating staircases, integrated air conditioning plenums, and traditional carved prayer rooms.

1 / 15

06. Key Internship Learnings

  • 1. Designing Within Pre-Existing Shells Working on Vaishnavi Serene’s precast structure reinforced that interior material palettes, service drops, and partition layouts must adapt to rigid structural envelopes.
  • 2. Embracing Existing Site Assets Integrating a mature tree into the heart of Toit’s atrium demonstrated how retaining site topography and natural vegetation elevates architectural character.
  • 3. Visualizations vs. Built Reality Conducting regular on-site inspections across stone cladding, structural roof trusses, and staircase installations connected 3D rendering directly with structural constructability.
  • 4. Multi-Sector GFC Execution Delivering comprehensive Good-For-Construction packages across hospitality venues, corporate lounges, and luxury residences sharpened drafting precision for direct on-site execution.
