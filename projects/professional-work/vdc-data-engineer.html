Architecting the spatial data pipelines that power automated construction analytics.

Raw design-phase BIM files are inherently uncoordinated across disciplines. I transformed fragmented Revit and CAD datasets across Architecture, Structure, MEP, and Fire Protection into unified, verified digital twins—managing coordinate harmonization, GUID deduplication, trade segmentation, and web-ready geometry extraction for backend ingestion.

01. System Pipeline

End-to-end integration from spatial intake to downstream deliverables. I structured the transformation of raw geometry (BIM meshes) and reality-capture data (scanned point clouds) into verified analytics models. Working with strict annotation standards, I coordinated with QA analysts to resolve geometric clashes and attribute errors before deploying standardized spatial assets for 4D timeline scheduling, automated quantity takeoffs (QTO), and cost reporting.

02. Data Pipeline

Every project adhered to a standardized 7‑step data protocol. I federated models across Architecture, Structure, MEP, and Fire Protection into a shared global coordinate system; executed automated QA/QC to reject upstream geometric anomalies; assigned unique hierarchical GUIDs to parent-child assemblies while eliminating duplicates; segmented geometry into functional discipline packages; assigned tracking units for quantity analysis; and pushed verified OBJ payloads and configuration tickets to the software development team.

05. Key Responsibilities

  • 1. Multi‑Trade Federation Consolidated disparate Revit, IFC, and CAD files from Architecture, Structure, MEP, and Fire Protection into a single verified coordinate baseline.
  • 2. Coordinate & Grid Calibration Aligned all files to a common coordinate system and matched 2D floor plan layout sheets to 3D models to prevent spatial drift.
  • 3. Upstream QA/QC Audit Audited incoming models for file corruption, unbound geometry, and missing parameters—rejecting faulty data upstream.
  • 4. GUID Hierarchy Validation Injected and verified persistent GUID values across parent and child components with zero duplicate entries across linked models.
  • 5. Trade Segmentation Partitioned complex digital twins into organized discipline sets (Arch, Struct, MEP) and spatial tracking zones per project scope.
  • 6. Unit Mapping & QTO Prep Assigned dimensional attributes and tracking units to components to ensure seamless automated quantity takeoffs and progress tracking.
  • 7. Backend Upload & Web Deployment Exported lightweight OBJ assets for browser visualization, established 2D layout templates, and configured backend database tickets.
  • 8. Cross-Functional Handoff Collaborated with software development and machine learning teams to ensure spatial schemas aligned with analytical requirements.

07. The Work Philosophy

My job was to take raw, uncoordinated trade models and turn them into a single, verified digital twin that the analytics engine could read. I handled the entire data pipeline — from aligning coordinates and assigning GUIDs, to removing duplicates, segmenting by trade, and exporting lightweight web models. I was the bridge between the design models and the backend development team. — I didn't build the AI. I built the clean, structured data that made the AI work.
