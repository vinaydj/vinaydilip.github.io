COMMERCIAL RETAIL MALL

Coastal Retail Development · Salt-Laden Wind Mitigation · 6m Structural Bay Grid

  • LocationTuticorin Beach Road, Tamil Nadu, India
  • ProgrammeBachelor of Architecture — Design Studio
  • ScopeMicroclimate Siting, Retail-Office Stacking, Service Separation, Façade Weathering Strategy
  • ToolsAutodesk Revit, SketchUp, AutoCAD, Adobe Photoshop, Lumion, InDesign

A two-storey commercial development engineered to withstand aggressive coastal weathering.

Sited adjacent to salt-harvesting beds near Tuticorin Beach Road, the project serves nearby residential and industrial communities. To resist corrosive coastal winds, the design adopts a compact rectangular volume featuring ground-floor retail beneath five independent first-floor office suites.

01. Site Context & Coastal Strategy

Climatic resilience driving form. Siting the development next to open salt pans made long-term durability the primary design parameter. Surfaces and junctions exposed to prevailing maritime winds were kept minimal, reducing salt build-up and envelope degradation.

1 / 4

02. Massing, Program & Service Logistics

Strict logistics separation. Customer traffic accesses retail entries along the main road, while deliveries and waste management are routed through a dedicated rear service road, keeping the public facade unobstructed.

03. Circulation Hierarchy & Plans

1 / 2

The ground floor arranges five independent retail units with dedicated storage. Above, the first floor mirrors this layout into five independent office suites sharing a centralized service core.

04. Envelope Detailing & Structural Grid

A repetitive 6-meter structural bay grid runs the length of the building, streamlining structural execution and weatherproofing. Glazing is minimized along the wind-exposed flanks while focused at the shaded entrance atrium.

05. Academic & Design Takeaways

  • 1. Climate-Driven Material Selection Designing adjacent to salt pans made corrosion resistance the core design parameter, guiding façade junctions and envelope detailing.
  • 2. Complete Front/Back Logistical Separation Routing deliveries to a dedicated rear yard preserved an uninterrupted, pedestrian-friendly shopping frontage.
  • 3. Modulated Structural Grid A consistent 6-meter structural bay aligned retail shopfronts and office partition modules cleanly with minimal structural transfer.
  • 4. Strategic Fenestration Placement Limiting openings along salt-wind exposures while opening the sheltered entrance corner balanced natural daylighting with envelope longevity.
