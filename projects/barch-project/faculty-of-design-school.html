FACULTY OF DESIGN CAMPUS

Academic Design Studio · Industrial Brownfield Adaptive Planning · Diagrid Atrium

  • LocationOld Mysore Lamps Factory, Tumkur Rd, Malleswaram, Bengaluru
  • ProgrammeBachelor of Architecture — Major Design Studio
  • ScopeMaster Zoning, Department-Lab Bridges, Central Diagrid Atrium, Covered Pergola Spine
  • ToolsAutodesk Revit, SketchUp, AutoCAD, Adobe Photoshop, Lumion, InDesign

Reimagining an industrial brownfield site as a 600-student integrated design faculty.

Sited on the historic 13-acre Mysore Lamps Factory plot along Tumkur Road, this institutional studio project mediates between high-density urban transit lines and public open space near Orion Mall. The architectural scheme organizes five design disciplines—Textile, Product, Interior, Graphic, and Fashion Design—into paired academic and laboratory wings linked by cross-bridges, framing a public ground realm with exhibition galleries and an amphitheater.

01. Site & Urban Context

Urban density and sightline mapping. Comprehensive analysis of pedestrian movement along Tumkur Road and Sandal Soap Factory Metro Station informed the placement of public entrances. Siting the primary gateway as a natural extension of civic foot traffic ensured seamless integration between public exhibition spaces and internal academic clusters.

1 / 3

02. Departmental Pairing & Massing

Pairing theory with hands-on fabrication. Each design discipline pairs an academic lecture block directly with its corresponding technical workshop across an enclosed glazed bridge. This programmatic pairing steps down toward a central administrative core, student housing, cafeteria, and a multi-level public exhibition hall.

03. Architectural Enclosure & Fins

A rhythm of deep vertical stone fins articulates the exterior academic envelope, mitigating harsh solar glare on eastern and western exposures while maintaining visual transparency. Dark arched portals create a welcoming pedestrian threshold from the public green into the shaded central courtyard.

04. Floor Plans & Spatial Zoning

1 / 4

The floor plans are structured around dual circulation cores: a western classroom wing and an eastern administrative and exhibition wing. Internal landscaped lightwells introduce natural daylight and passive stack ventilation into the center of deep studio floor plates across all levels.

05. Sections, Elevations & Diagrid Atrium

The diagrid-topped central dome. Section B-B illustrates the focal spatial move of the project: a steel-and-glass diagrid atrium that channels diffuse zenithal daylight into the administrative core while anchoring the skyline of the campus.

06. Colonnade & Shaded Walkways

A continuous pergola spine connects the five academic pavilions. Its repeating structural steel frames provide weather-protected routes for pedestrians and cyclists while creating dynamic shadow patterns throughout the day.

07. Academic & Design Takeaways

  • 1. Transit-Oriented Site Planning Aligning primary entrance plazas with existing pedestrian desires from the adjacent metro station created an active, permeable public threshold[cite: 7].
  • 2. Studio-to-Workshop Integration Connecting design classrooms directly to fabrication laboratories via glazed cross-bridges streamlined practical workflows and defined the stepped massing hierarchy[cite: 7].
  • 3. Central Diagrid Civic Core Introducing a daylight-flooded diagrid atrium provided an unmistakable orientation landmark across the broader 13-acre campus footprint[cite: 7].
  • 4. Solar Envelope Modulation Carrying a calibrated vertical stone fin module across façades and covered walkways unified diverse building scales under a cohesive institutional aesthetic[cite: 7].
