07. Practice Learnings & Project Takeaways

  • 1. End-to-End Client & Site Ownership Directly managing projects from concept sketches through material procurement and contractor coordination developed comprehensive accountability across cost and build quality.
  • 2. Precision in Joinery & Millwork GFCs Drafting 1:10 and 1:20 scale shop drawings for custom acoustic paneling, floating beds, and modular kitchens eliminated on-site fabrication ambiguity.
  • 3. Space Optimization on Tight Infill Sites Designing multi-tier mixed-use schemes (retail + residential) required balancing structural grids with independent service cores on compact urban footprints.
  • 4. High-Fidelity Design Communication Pairing detailed GFC drawings with photorealistic renderings ensured that clients approved spatial volumes and lighting temperatures accurately prior to construction mobilization.
